Problems I Help Solve

Your Business Can Be Good and Still Be Difficult to Find Online.

You can have great products, excellent services, and years of experience. But if people cannot find your business online, they may never discover you.

"I have a website, but people aren't finding it."

Your website may be live, but important pages may not be properly optimized or indexed.

"My business isn't showing on Google."

Your website or Google Business Profile may need better optimization.

"I've been posting, but I'm not getting enquiries."

Being active online doesn't automatically mean you're reaching the people searching for what you offer.

"My website looks good, but it isn't helping my business."

Your website needs to communicate your services clearly and give visitors a reason to take the next step.

"My website project keeps getting delayed."

Unclear requirements, scope changes, missing information, and poor communication can affect delivery.

"Nobody is coordinating everything."

Developers, designers, SEO specialists, and stakeholders all need to stay aligned. I coordinate the moving parts.
