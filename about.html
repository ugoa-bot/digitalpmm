About

My name is Ugochinyere Amaonyeanaso.

I'm a Digital Project Manager working across websites, software, mobile apps, SEO, and marketing projects.

I also work directly with business owners who need help with their websites, SEO, Google Business Profile, and online visibility.

I don't just manage the task. I look at the problem the project is supposed to solve.

A website can be delivered on time and still fail to support the business. An SEO campaign can generate traffic without generating the right enquiries. A software project can be completed without properly solving the original business requirement.

That's why I look at the business goal, project requirements, stakeholders, timeline, technical work, SEO, quality, and final outcome.
